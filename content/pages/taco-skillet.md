title: Taco Skillet
tags: main dishes

**Ingredients**

* ⅔ cup uncooked rice (jasmine, basmati, or long-grain white rice)
* 1⅓ cups water
* 1 lb lean ground beef
* 1 small yellow onion, diced (about 1 cup)
* 1 medium red bell pepper, diced (about 1 cup)
* 1 medium zucchini, diced (about 1 cup)
* 1 (15-ounce) can low-sodium black beans, drained and rinsed
* 1 (16-ounce) jar of salsa
* 1 taco seasoning packet (about ¼ cup)
* 8 ounces shredded cheese, divided
* Fresh chopped cilantro, cherry tomatoes, sour cream, and lime wedges for garnish (optional)

**Directions**

1. Combine the rice and water in a 2-quart saucepan. Stir and bring to a boil, uncovered, over medium-high heat.
   Reduce the heat to low, cover, and cook for 18 minutes. Remove from the heat and let stand, covered, for
   5 minutes. Fluff the rice with a wooden spoon.
2. Meanwhile, heat a 3.9-quart sauté pan over medium-high heat. Add the ground beef and onion. Cook for
   4–5 minutes, breaking up the beef with a wooden spatula, until the onion has softened.
3. Stir in the bell pepper and zucchini. Cook for another 3–4 minutes, until the beef is cooked through and
   no longer pink.
4. Reduce the heat to medium-low. Stir in the black beans, salsa, taco seasoning, and 3 ounces of the shredded
   cheese. Gently fold in the cooked rice until evenly combined.
5. Sprinkle the remaining 5 ounces of cheese over the top. Cover and cook over low heat for 15–18 minutes,
   until the skillet is heated through and the cheese has melted.
6. Remove from the heat and uncover. Garnish with cilantro, cherry tomatoes, sour cream, and lime wedges, if desired.
