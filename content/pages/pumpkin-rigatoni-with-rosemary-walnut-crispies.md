title: Pumpkin Rigatoni with Rosemary Walnut Crispies
tags: main dishes

Lindsay Ostrom / Pinch of Yum

Prep Time: 15 minutes  
Cook Time: 30 minutes  
Total Time: 45 minutes  
Yield: 6 servings

**Ingredients**

Pumpkin Pasta:

* 1 lb. rigatoni
* 2 tbsp butter
* 4 cloves garlic, thinly sliced or minced
* 1 cup pumpkin puree
* 1 cup chicken or vegetable broth
* 1 cup heavy cream
* 1–2 tsp kosher salt
* Squeeze of fresh lemon juice

Rosemary Walnut Crispies:

* 1 cup walnuts, finely chopped
* 1 tbsp fresh rosemary leaves, finely chopped
* 1 tbsp melted butter
* 1–2 tbsp brown sugar
* 1/2 tsp kosher salt (plus more to taste)
* Freshly ground black pepper, to taste

**Directions**

1. Make the Walnut Crispies: Preheat oven to 375°F. Chop the walnuts in a food processor until evenly textured. Spread
   on a small sheet pan and toast for 8–10 minutes. Remove from the oven and toss with the rosemary, melted butter,
   brown sugar, salt, and pepper. Set aside to cool.
2. Cook the Pasta: Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook rigatoni according to package directions.
   Drain.
3. Make the Pumpkin Sauce: While the pasta cooks, melt 2 tablespoons of butter in a skillet over low heat. Add garlic
   and sauté until fragrant (do not let it brown). Stir in the pumpkin puree and broth; simmer until smooth. Stir in
   the heavy cream and salt, then simmer until sauce thickens and coats the back of a spoon. Finish with a squeeze of
   fresh lemon juice, then toss with the cooked pasta.
4. Assemble & Serve: Portion pasta into shallow bowls and top generously with the rosemary walnut crispies. Garnish
   with a sprig of fresh rosemary if desired.

**Variations**

Vegan Option: Swap the butter for olive oil (or plant butter) and the heavy cream for full-fat coconut milk.
